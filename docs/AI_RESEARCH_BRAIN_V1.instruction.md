# Instruction — AI_RESEARCH_BRAIN_V1.md

**Instruction ID:** INST-AI-BRAIN
**Target:** `docs/AI_RESEARCH_BRAIN_V1.md`
**Class:** A — Critical
**Status:** ACTIVE

## Purpose
Govern the project-specific AI research intelligence layer while keeping research data, evidence, memory, and rules independent of any model/provider.

## Rules
- AI is a research assistant, not the final authority.
- Evidence and verification status must remain explicit.
- Provider/model changes must not damage Research Core assets.
- Do not turn speculative AI output into verified research data automatically.
- Preserve stable IDs, research memory, audit history, and provider-independent architecture.
- Changes to AI architecture must be checked against API, website, data, and continuity dependencies.

## Dependencies
`MASTER_INSTRUCTION.md`, `docs/AI_RESEARCH_AGENT_SYSTEM_V1.md`, `docs/RESEARCH_CHAIN_OF_COMMAND.md`, research data/schema, backend/API, chat UI.

## Verification
Test both conceptual consistency and actual integration points before declaring an AI architecture change complete.

## Update Trigger
Changes to AI responsibilities, research workflow, evidence/verification handling, memory, tool control, provider abstraction, or AI integration architecture.
